ABOUT OUR SHOP

A Little Tradition in Every Bite

Devakirubai Sweets and Bakery is a local bakery and sweet shop located on Cathedral Street in Nazareth, Thoothukudi district.

The shop is known locally for mixture, kara sev and traditional native snacks, along with sweets and bakery favourites.

Planning a family function or bulk purchase? Call the shop directly to discuss quantities, availability and other arrangements.
